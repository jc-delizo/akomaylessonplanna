## Discussion Areas

Please lead us through finalizing decisions for the following areas:

### 1. Social Sharing Buttons
- Where should share buttons appear? (Product pages, seller profiles, homepage)
- Which platforms to support? (Facebook, Messenger, Twitter/X, Pinterest, WhatsApp, email)
- Share button UI style (icons, text buttons, both?)
- Copy link to clipboard functionality
- QR code sharing for mobile

### 2. Share Content Customization
- What gets shared? (Product image, title, price, seller name, description preview)
- Open Graph metadata for social previews
- Custom message input for user to add their own message
- UTM tracking for share analytics

### 3. Recently Viewed Items
- Where should recently viewed appear? (Sidebar on product page, dedicated page only—no homepage section.)
- How many items to track? (10, 20, 50?)
- Persistence behavior (session-only, 7 days, 30 days, forever?)
- Anonymous users (track via localStorage? require login?)
- Privacy option (opt-out of tracking?)

### 4. Notification System
This is a MAJOR component. Types to consider:
- **Seller Notifications:**
  - New sale (already in Feature 04)
  - New review (already in Feature 05)
  - New follower (from Feature 02)
  - Product update published
  - Withdrawal completed
  - Low stock alerts (for digital, not needed)

- **Buyer Notifications:**
  - Price drop on wishlist items
  - Seller you follow uploaded new product
  - Seller responded to your review
  - Cart abandonment (already in Feature 04)
  - Review reminder (already in Feature 05)

- **System Notifications:**
  - Platform announcements
  - Maintenance notices
  - Feature updates
  - Policy changes

**Notification Delivery:**
- In-app notifications (bell icon with count)
- Email notifications (digest vs immediate)
- Push notifications (PWA, future feature?)
- SMS (not needed for MVP)

**Notification Preferences:**
- Per-type toggles (user can choose which to receive)
- Frequency controls (immediate, daily digest, weekly digest, off)
- Email vs in-app selection

### 5. Activity Feed
- Should we have a "Feed" of activity? (Similar to Facebook/Twitter)
  - Show followed sellers' new products
  - Show reviews from people you follow (if we add following buyers)
  - Show system announcements
- Or keep it simple (notifications only, no feed)?

### 6. "Tell a Friend" / Share your purchase (Out of scope for MVP)
- Deferred: email sharing and order-confirmation share CTA.

### 7. Social Proof Elements
- "X people viewing this product now" (real-time counter)
- "X people added this to wishlist" (wishlist count visible)
- "Recently purchased by Teacher Maria M." (social proof on product page)
- "Trending in Grade 7 Math" (category trends)

### 8. Gift Purchases (Future Feature)
- Buy product as gift for another teacher
- Gift wrapping/digital card
- Scheduled delivery
- **Decision:** Include in MVP or defer to post-launch?

### 9. Community Features (Future)
- Teacher forums/discussion boards
- Q&A section for products
- Teacher groups/communities
- **Decision:** Include in MVP or defer to post-launch?

## Key Questions to Resolve

1. **Notification Architecture:** In-app + email, or email-only for MVP?
2. **Recently Viewed:** Track for everyone or logged-in users only?
3. **Share Platforms:** Which ones are essential for Filipino teachers? (Facebook, Messenger must-haves?)
4. **Activity Feed:** Worth the complexity, or keep it simple with notifications?
5. **Social Proof Display:** Real-time counters (expensive) or static badges (simpler)?
6. **Gift Purchases:** MVP or post-launch?
7. **Referral Tracking:** Implement now or later for reward program?

---

## Design Considerations

**Filipino Teacher Behavior:**
- Heavy Facebook/Messenger usage (80%+ penetration)
- Love sharing discoveries with colleagues
- Trust peer recommendations highly
- Active in teacher Facebook groups
- Mobile-first usage

**Technical Constraints:**
- Real-time features (view counters) require WebSockets/Server-Sent Events
- Push notifications require service workers (PWA)
- Email delivery reliability critical
- Notification spam risk (need smart defaults)

**Privacy:**
- Recently viewed = potentially sensitive
- Notification preferences = user control
- Social proof = anonymized ("Teacher Maria", not "Maria Santos")
